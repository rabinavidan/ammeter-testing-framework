How a test lands in a pod

Sharding

Each test-run pod gets an index from the Indexed Job (JOB_COMPLETION_INDEX). tests/conftest.py sorts the selected test IDs and keeps every N-th one, starting at its index. Shards never overlap, together cover the whole selection, and differ by at most one test. Each shard uploads its Allure results to the collector, which builds one merged report when the last shard arrives.
